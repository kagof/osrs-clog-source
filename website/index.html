OSRS Collection Log Source

Backed by data from the OSRS Wiki (though not associated with or endorsed by the OSRS Wiki, Jagex, or Old School RuneScape).

Although the Wiki is an amazing source of of information about OSRS, someone motivated by filling out niche entries in their Collection Log may struggle to find the info they need. For example, on receiving a slayer task for an unfamiliar monster, it is difficult to find out whether that monster has any Collection Log drops from the Wiki article. Searching this webpage for an Old School RuneScape activity (boss, monster, minigame, etc) returns the Collection Log items associated with that activity.
